Home Assistant (MQTT)

Uses your Home Assistant MQTT broker (e.g. the Mosquitto add-on). LEDBoard then appears under Settings → Devices as LEDBoard with a Display light, Board selector, Pause, Next, Message and an Alert notify entity.

Send an alert

Alerts interrupt the display with an animated icon. Critical alerts repeat every minute (and wake the display) until dismissed. Home Assistant can send them through MQTT or POST /api/alert.
